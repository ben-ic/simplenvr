Bundled Third-Party Binaries
============================

In addition to FFmpeg (see FFMPEG_CREDITS.txt), SimpleNVR distributes
the following unmodified third-party binaries as Tauri sidecars.

go2rtc
------
go2rtc is a real-time streaming server used by SimpleNVR as a single
RTSP client per camera. SimpleNVR's recording and motion FFmpeg
processes connect to go2rtc's local loopback rather than directly to
each camera, so the camera only sees one RTSP connection regardless
of how many internal consumers SimpleNVR runs.

License: MIT (a copy of the upstream LICENSE is bundled at
         resources/go2rtc-LICENSE)
Upstream: https://github.com/AlexxIT/go2rtc
Bundled version: v1.9.14

SimpleNVR uses go2rtc exclusively as a subprocess and does not link
to it at compile or load time.
